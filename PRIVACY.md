# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 12, 2026

League Client Companion is a desktop companion tool for League of Legends. This
application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, store,
transmit, or share any personal user data, telemetry, or analytics. All local
configuration data remains strictly on your machine.

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at
<https://github.com/tilwinjoy/league-companion-app/issues>.
