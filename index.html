League Companion App

Track your champion collection, mastery progress, skins and a live wishlist sales radar - all in one focused dashboard that reads your game data from the local League client.

Checking the latest release…

Take a look inside

A single window for your whole collection. Use the theme switch above to preview both dark and light modes.

Dashboard

Collection and skin progress, mastery score, Blue Essence and an at-a-glance shop & sales radar.

Champion inventory

Every champion with skins owned, mastery level and the points left to the next level.

Wishlist & sales radar

Pin the skins you want and get notified the moment they go on sale or land in the Mythic Shop.

Built for the collection grind

Everything you need to plan the next unlock - without a bloated toolkit.

Hextech Shard Engine

Grades every shard and skin in your loot, showing which disenchants close a skin gap and which are worth keeping - so no Orange Essence goes to waste.

Persistent Filters

Search, sort and filter the champion inventory and skin browser; your choices are remembered between sessions, so you pick up exactly where you left off.

Custom Matchup Vaults

Keep private, per-champion notes and matchup references in your own vaults, ready before you lock in your next game.

Lightweight Electron/React build

A slim Electron + React shell that reads the local League client and stays light on resources - no bloat and no background services.
