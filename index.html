Wishlists & Smart Sales Radar

Never miss your dream skin or Prestige drop again. Pin any skin, chroma, or cosmetic to your per-account wishlist and receive real-time desktop alerts the exact moment it rotates into weekly store sales, drops in price, or hits the Mythic Shop.

Skins Browser, Community Rankings & Spotlights

Explore the complete cosmetic catalog filtered by champion, rarity, tier, and ownership state. Dive deep into any skin to view its community poll rankings and embedded SkinSpotlights videos, allowing you to preview in-game animations and see exactly how the community rates it.

Champion Roster & Mastery Explorer

Deep-dive inventory covering every champion, mastery tier, milestone progression, and points-to-next-level tracking. Outfitted with robust multi-criteria filtering and grouping options—such as least skins owned, most skins owned, mastery progressions, and so on.
